Chemistry & plant protection (high level)

  • Know what you’re treating (algae, mold, oxidation) and surface type before choosing mix strength.
  • Keep SDS accessible; train anyone who mixes or sprays.
  • Plan rinse strategy for sensitive plants, painted surfaces, and neighboring properties.
  • Store chemicals securely; respect temperature and container compatibility.
  • Never invent “secret mix recipes” from blogs as gospel — follow reputable training and manufacturer guidance.

PPE & truck essentials checklist

  1. Eye protection and chemical-resistant gloves
  2. Appropriate clothing / boots; consider respirator guidance per SDS
  3. Spill kit and neutralizing / rinse plan
  4. GFCI and electrical safety if using powered accessories
  5. Cone/signage kit for driveways and sidewalks
  6. Camera or phone mounts for before/after documentation
  7. First aid kit and emergency contacts

Starter budget mindset (no invented prices)

Build a written BOM (bill of materials) with three quotes for pump, tanks, hose/reel, and injection. Include tax, mounting fabrication, and spare parts. Leave contingency for fittings and unexpected adapters. Verify every price on supplier sites the day you buy.

FAQ

Can I soft wash with a regular pressure washer?

Some operators start with downstream injection on a pressure washer. Dedicated soft wash setups are often preferred for roofs and large surfaces. Choose based on training, surfaces you’ll treat, and manufacturer guidance — not a one-line internet tip.

What’s the #1 beginner mistake?

Underestimating plant protection, rinse water, and chemical safety — or buying gear with no local parts support. Training matters as much as equipment.
