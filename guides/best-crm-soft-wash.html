What “best CRM” means for soft wash

“Best” is the tool your team will actually use every day. Soft wash ops usually need:

  • Package-based estimates (house wash, roof, concrete, gutters) with optional upsells
  • Job notes & photos for stain types, plant protection, HOA rules, and gate codes
  • Scheduling that survives weather — quick moves and customer texts
  • Recurring / annual clean reminders for retention
  • Crew mobile access without giving everyone full admin rights
  • Fast invoicing before you leave the driveway

Buying criteria checklist

  1. Can you build 5–10 reusable soft wash packages in under an hour?
  2. Can a tech complete a job, add photos, and collect payment from a phone?
  3. Can you reschedule a full day of rain-outs without rebuilding the calendar?
  4. Does reporting show revenue by service type (roof vs house vs concrete)?
  5. What’s the all-in monthly cost including users and payment fees?
  6. Is there an exit path (export customers/jobs) if you outgrow it?

FAQ

Is QuickBooks enough instead of a CRM?

QuickBooks helps accounting. Soft wash still needs scheduling, customer job history, and field quotes. Many shops use a field-service CRM plus accounting software.

Should I wait until I have employees?

If you’re drowning in texts and unpaid invoices as a solo, start earlier. If volume is low, a lightweight tool may be enough until you hire.

How long should a trial last?

Run at least 10–20 real jobs through the tool — quotes, schedule changes, and payments — before you decide.
