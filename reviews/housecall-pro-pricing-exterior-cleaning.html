Housecall Pro Pricing Explained for Exterior Cleaning

Updated September 2026 · Pricing context (not a live quote) · Affiliate disclosure

Housecall Pro (HCP) sells subscription plans that bundle CRM, scheduling, payments, and (on higher tiers) marketing and sales tools. Exterior cleaning contractors often ask: “What will I actually pay?” The honest answer: it depends on plan tier, users/add-ons, and card processing fees — and public list prices change. This page explains how to evaluate cost for a pressure washing or soft wash shop without inventing dollar figures.

How HCP pricing is typically structured

Public materials generally describe multiple plan tiers. Exact names and inclusions evolve, but evaluation usually includes:

Cost component What to ask Why it matters for wash businesses
Base plan subscription Monthly vs annual; what’s included at each tier Marketing/phone features may only sit on higher tiers
Users / employees How many logins; tech-only vs admin seats Multi-truck soft wash crews add seats quickly
Payment processing Card rates, ACH options, payout timing High ticket house/roof jobs amplify fee impact
Add-ons Extra phone lines, marketing credits, hardware Easy to overbuy relative to lead volume
Contract terms Cancellation, minimums, equipment leases Read before you depend on the stack mid-season

Value checklist for exterior cleaning

  • Will you use marketing tools? If most jobs come from Google Business Profile and referrals, a lower operational tier may be enough.
  • Do you need sales pipeline / call tracking? Useful for inbound-heavy shops; optional for fully booked seasonal washers.
  • Mobile job completion: Confirm techs can finish invoices on-site — that’s where cash-flow ROI shows up.
  • Compare apples-to-apples with Jobber: See Jobber vs Housecall Pro and Jobber pricing notes.

Pros & cons of paying for HCP

Potential upsides

  • Consolidates ops + growth tools
  • Strong brand recognition among home-service pros
  • Partner materials sometimes highlight exterior cleaning niches

Watch-outs

  • Higher tiers can exceed what a small wash company needs
  • Processing fees are easy to underestimate
  • Switching mid-busy-season is painful — trial first

Who HCP pricing is (and isn’t) for

  • Good fit to evaluate: Growing exterior cleaning companies that want CRM + marketing in one vendor and will use the features they’re paying for.
  • Maybe overkill: Occasional side-gig washers who only need invoices and a calendar.
