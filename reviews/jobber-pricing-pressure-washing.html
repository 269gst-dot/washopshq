ROI framing for wash companies

Jobber (or any CRM) pays for itself when it reduces leakage:

  • Quotes that never get followed up
  • No-shows from missing reminders
  • Jobs completed without invoices sent same day
  • Lost annual re-clean revenue from missing customer history

Estimate: if better follow-up wins even a small number of house-wash jobs per month, software cost is often justified — but run your own numbers.

Pros & cons (pricing perspective)

Why crews pay for Jobber

  • Clear ops workflow: request → quote → job → invoice
  • Widely documented for home-service businesses
  • Mobile field tools support driveway close-outs

Cost watch-outs

  • Per-user growth can surprise multi-crew shops
  • Card fees stack on top of subscription
  • Paying for unused marketing features wastes margin

Who should evaluate Jobber pricing carefully

  • Good candidates: Pressure washing businesses quoting weekly, needing professional estimates and faster payment collection.
  • Maybe wait: Ultra-low volume side hustles still learning pricing — a simple invoice app may be enough temporarily.

FAQ

Does Jobber offer annual discounts?

Many SaaS vendors do, but terms change. Ask Jobber directly or check the live pricing page for current annual vs monthly options.

Is there an affiliate program?

Jobber markets an affiliates program (often via PartnerStack). Approval and commission terms are controlled by Jobber/PartnerStack — apply on their official affiliate pages.
